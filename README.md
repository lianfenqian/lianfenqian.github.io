# DATA 498D Senior Capstone

## Course Description

A culminating “capstone” experience that synthesizes concepts and skills for learning from data, as accumulated over the students’ experience in the Data Science major. We will introduce students to activities that will round out their undergraduate experience, including concepts in reproducible research, ethics in statistical and data science practice, high-performance computing, written and oral communication skills, working in teams, and professional development. Students will engage in projects and work to complete their academic portfolio.

## Course Format and Teaching Methods

The course meets in-person three times per week. It utilizes “flip classroom” format. Students complete their work through Brightspace. Access to all course materials such as readings, reading assignments, websites, or videos is provided on the Brightspace course site. Students may not modify content or re-use content for any purpose other than personal educational reasons. Students accessing unauthorized materials or using them in a manner inconsistent with UofA values and educational policies are subject to suspension. All recordings and materials are the property of the faculty members.

## Course Objectives

Upon completion of this course, students should be able to:
- Complement their statistical and data science skills with modern internet hosting services for software development and version controlling.
- Understand the obligations necessary for the ethical practice of data science.
- Improve their oral and written communication skills.
- Review and improve past projects and add a substantial new project to their academic portfolio.
- Work collaboratively on a substantial data intensive project.
- Become more prepared for the next steps for a career in industry, government, or academia.

## Assignments

There are 3 components to your overall grade:

- Class Activities and Discussions (30% of course grade): This class is largely a discussion-based class. Active participation in all discussions and in course activities is expected. These will be graded on participation and correctness.
- Portfolio (20% of course grade): Part of becoming a professional includes creating and maintaining a professional portfolio. Throughout the semester you will work on creating and adding things to your portfolio. This will be submitted at the end of the semester for evaluation. Feedback opportunities will be provided throughout the semester.
- Projects (50% of course grade): This course includes two projects that will be completed throughout the semester.
- Project 1 (20% of course grade): This is an individual project to update and improve upon a project that you completed earlier in your academic career. The components to this project include
  - Reflection Essay (50% of the project grade) – A reflection on your initial work.
  - Poster (50% of the project grade) – Created based on the updates to the initial work.
- Project 2 (30% of course grade): This project will be completed in groups of 2-3 students. The components to this project include
  - Proposal (5% of project grade)
  - Midterm Report (15% of project grade)
  - Final Report (30% of project grade)
  - Slides and Presentation (50% of project grade)
